# Awesome German Pragmatics [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

> A curated list of resources for German pragmatics, covering contextual language use, politeness strategies, and discourse analysis.

Pragmatics studies how context contributes to meaning in German language use. This includes understanding politeness conventions, speech acts, conversational implicature, and how Germans communicate in different social situations.

## Academic Resources

### Books

- [Handbuch der deutschen Konnektoren](https://www.degruyter.com/document/doi/10.1515/9783110341447/html) - Comprehensive handbook on German connectors and their pragmatic functions.
- [Höflichkeit im Deutschen](https://www.narr.de/) - Detailed analysis of politeness strategies in German communication.
- [Deutsche Gesprächsanalyse](https://www.narr-shop.de/) - Introduction to conversation analysis for German discourse.
- [Pragmatik der deutschen Sprache](https://www.springer.com/) - Foundational text on German pragmatics covering key concepts and applications.
- [Abtönungspartikeln](https://www.degruyter.com/) - Study of modal particles and their pragmatic uses in German.

### Research Papers

- [Politeness Phenomena in Germany](https://www.cambridge.org/) - Cross-cultural comparison of politeness strategies.
- [Speech Acts in German Requests](https://journals.sagepub.com/) - Analysis of request formulations and their social implications.
- [Du vs. Sie: The T-V Distinction in Modern German](https://www.tandfonline.com/) - Contemporary usage patterns of formal and informal address.

### Journals

- [Zeitschrift für germanistische Linguistik](https://www.degruyter.com/journal/key/zgl/html) - Leading journal for German linguistics including pragmatics.
- [Journal of Pragmatics](https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/journal-of-pragmatics) - International journal often featuring German language studies.
- [Gesprächsforschung](http://www.gespraechsforschung-ozs.de/) - Online journal dedicated to conversation analysis in German.

## Online Courses and Tutorials

## Tools and Applications

- [German-English Dictionary App](https://german-english-dictionary.devtheworld.jp/) - For scientifically-efficient modern learning.
- [DWDS - Digitales Wörterbuch der deutschen Sprache](https://www.dwds.de/) - Digital dictionary with usage examples and collocations.
- [DeReKo - Deutsches Referenzkorpus](https://www.ids-mannheim.de/digspra/kospra/korpora/dereko/) - Reference corpus for studying real German language use.
- [Grammis](https://grammis.ids-mannheim.de/) - Grammar information system with pragmatic annotations.

## Corpora and Databases

- [FOLK - Forschungs- und Lehrkorpus](https://dgd.ids-mannheim.de/) - Research and teaching corpus of spoken German with rich annotations.
- [GeWiss - Gesprochene Wissenschaftssprache](https://gewiss.uni-leipzig.de/) - Corpus of spoken academic German.
- [Korpus Südtirol](https://www.korpus-suedtirol.it/) - Corpus including South Tyrolean German varieties.
- [C-ORAL-ROM German](http://www.lablita.it/corpora/coralrom/) - Comparable corpus of spontaneous speech.

## Politeness and Formality

### Du vs. Sie

- [T-V Distinction Guide](https://www.goethe.de/) - When to use formal "Sie" vs. informal "du".
- [Regional Variations in Address Forms](https://www.atlas-alltagssprache.de/) - Interactive atlas showing regional differences.
- [Corporate Du: Modern Trends](https://www.deutschland.de/) - Changing formality norms in German workplaces.

### Modal Particles

- [Abtönungspartikeln Guide](https://www.germanveryeasy.com/) - Understanding "doch", "mal", "ja", etc. in context.
- [Modal Particles in Requests](https://www.deutsch-lernen.com/) - How particles soften or strengthen speech acts.

## Speech Acts

- [Making Requests in German](https://www.thoughtco.com/) - Different strategies and formality levels.
- [Apologies and Excuses](https://www.fluentu.com/german/) - Cultural norms around apologizing in German.
- [Compliments and Responses](https://www.germanculture.com.ua/) - How to give and receive compliments appropriately.
- [Refusals and Disagreements](https://www.transparent.com/german/) - Polite ways to say no in German.

## Discourse Analysis

- [Turn-Taking in German Conversation](https://www.degruyter.com/) - Patterns of conversational organization.
- [Narrative Structures](https://www.narr.de/) - How Germans tell stories and construct narratives.
- [Argumentation Patterns](https://www.springer.com/) - Discourse strategies in German debates and discussions.

## Regional Variation

- [Austrian German Pragmatics](https://www.oesterreichisches-deutsch.at/) - Pragmatic differences in Austrian German.
- [Swiss German Communication](https://www.schweizerdeutsch.ch/) - Pragmatic features of Swiss German varieties.
- [Northern vs. Southern German Communication Styles](https://www.atlas-alltagssprache.de/) - Regional differences in directness and politeness.
